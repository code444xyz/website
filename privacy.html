Privacy

Last updated October 2026

This policy covers the code444.com.au website, operated by CODE444 PTY LTD. Each Code444 app has its own privacy policy describing exactly what that app collects, which you'll find in the app and on its App Store listing.

What this website collects

Nothing. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or forms. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

If you email us

We use your email address and whatever you choose to send only to reply to you. We don't add you to mailing lists, and we don't sell or share your details. Email is handled through Google Workspace.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
